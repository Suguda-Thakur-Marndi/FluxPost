# Page Design System: Workflow Page (/workflow)

> **Inherits from:** `design-system/media-scheduler/MASTER.md`  
> **Rule:** Only deviations, specific layout structures, and component placements for Workflow are defined here.

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Mental Model
The Workflow page demystifies the entire social media publishing pipeline. It educates potential users on how Media Scheduler saves 10+ hours per week by taking ideas seamlessly from initial brainstorming to automated multi-channel delivery.

---

## 2. Layout Structure

1. **Header:**
   - Pre-badge: "The Publishing Pipeline"
   - Title: "How Media Scheduler powers your content machine"
   - Subtitle: "From raw spark to published post across 8 networks in 3 streamlined steps."
2. **Interactive Step-by-Step Sections (Alternating Layout):**
   - **Step 1: Ideate & Capture**
     - Icon: Lightbulb / Brain
     - Features: Kanban idea boards, AI prompt generator, image attachment dropzones
     - Visual: Interactive preview card of Idea Kanban
   - **Step 2: Compose & Adapt**
     - Icon: Sparkles / Sliders
     - Features: Platform character limits, tone adapting, realistic preview switcher
     - Visual: Interactive preview card of Composer with live previews
   - **Step 3: Schedule & Auto-Publish**
     - Icon: Calendar / Send
     - Features: Drag-and-drop calendar slots, queue vs time slots, Inngest background publishing
     - Visual: Interactive preview of Calendar grid
3. **Productivity Benefits Metrics:**
   - "10+ Hours Saved Weekly", "0 Missed Deadlines", "8 Networks Synchronized"
4. **Bottom CTA Banner:**
   - "Experience the new way to publish."
