# Page Design System: Pricing Page (/pricing)

> **Inherits from:** `design-system/media-scheduler/MASTER.md`  
> **Rule:** Only deviations, specific layout structures, and component placements for Pricing are defined here.

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Mental Model
The Pricing page gives prospective and existing customers crystal-clear clarity regarding plan features, channel limits, and billing frequencies (Monthly vs Annual with 20% discount).

---

## 2. Layout Structure

1. **Header:**
   - Title: "Simple, transparent pricing for every creator & team"
   - Subtitle: "Choose the plan that fits your publishing cadence. Upgrade or cancel anytime."
   - Monthly / Yearly Billing Toggle with "Save 20%" badge.
2. **Pricing Grid (3 Cards):**
   - **Free Plan ($0/mo):**
     - Target: Solopreneurs and new creators
     - Features: 3 scheduled posts/mo, 2 connected channels, basic preview mode, idea board
     - CTA: "Get Started Free"
   - **Pro Plan ($12/mo, or $10/mo billed annually) [Highlighted]:**
     - Badge: "Most Popular"
     - Target: Active creators and small business owners
     - Features: Unlimited posts, all 8 channels, AI content assistant, multi-image carousel scheduling, calendar & list views
     - Accent: 2px primary border, elevated shadow, primary CTA button
     - CTA: "Start 14-Day Free Trial"
   - **Team Plan ($39/mo, or $32/mo billed annually):**
     - Target: Agencies and marketing teams
     - Features: Unlimited posts, unlimited channels, multi-workspace collaboration, priority queue, advanced analytics
     - CTA: "Start Team Trial"
3. **Feature Comparison Table:**
   - Comprehensive matrix comparing features across Free, Pro, and Team tiers.
4. **FAQ Accordion:**
   - Clean collapsible answers to common billing and channel connection questions.
5. **Bottom CTA Banner:**
   - "Have custom enterprise requirements? Talk to us."
