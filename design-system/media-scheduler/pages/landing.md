# Page Design System: Marketing Landing Page (/ and /landing)

> **Inherits from:** `design-system/media-scheduler/MASTER.md`  
> **Rule:** Only deviations, specific layout structures, and component placements for the Landing Page are defined here.

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Mental Model
The Landing Page introduces Media Scheduler to content creators, marketing teams, agencies, and small businesses. It establishes authority, demonstrates real workflow value, and converts visitors into active users through high-clarity typography, interactive product mockups, and transparent value propositions.

Avoid: Cheesy AI floating blobs, saturated neon gradients, and vague buzzwords.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Conversion Flow

1. **Sticky Top Navigation:**
   - Brand logo
   - Navigation links: Features, Workflow, Channels, Pricing
   - CTA buttons: "Log In" (Ghost) + "Start Creating" (Primary)
2. **Hero Section:**
   - Pre-title pill badge: "Next-Gen Multi-Channel Publishing"
   - H1: "Plan, create and publish your social content from one workspace."
   - Subtitle: "Coordinate Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, YouTube, and TikTok with an intuitive calendar, AI drafting, and pixel-perfect previews."
   - Dual CTAs: Primary "Start Creating" + Secondary "See How It Works"
   - Social platform logo strip: 8 official platform icons in muted monochrome/subtle color.
3. **Interactive Product Showcase:**
   - Realistic dashboard & calendar UI preview mockup with interactive channel switcher or tab preview.
4. **Key Feature Grid (2x2 or 3-column):**
   - AI Content Generation
   - Multi-Platform Visual Calendar
   - Channel-Specific Customization & Limits
   - Idea Kanban Board
5. **3-Step Workflow:**
   - 01 Ideate -> 02 Adapt & Compose -> 03 Schedule & Publish
6. **Supported Platforms Showcase:**
   - Card grid for 8 networks highlighting API integrations, character limits, and media rules.
7. **Customer Testimonials & Social Proof:**
   - Metric counters (e.g. "99.9% On-time Publishing", "8+ Supported Networks")
   - Authentic quote cards from social media managers and agency leads.
8. **Final High-Impact CTA Banner:**
   - "Ready to take back hours every week?" + "Get Started Free" button.
9. **Footer:**
   - Brand logo, company links, legal links (Privacy, Terms), and copyright.
